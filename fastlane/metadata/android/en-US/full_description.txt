Wishday remembers birthdays and helps you send the message.

<ul>
<li>Import birthdays from your contacts or a Birday backup, or add them by hand</li>
<li>Auto SMS: Wishday texts them at the time you choose, and retries if it fails</li>
<li>Signal, Molly or WhatsApp: a notification opens the chat with your message ready, so you just tap send</li>
<li>Star important people and get an early gift reminder, with your gift ideas</li>
<li>Stats: birthdays by month, big birthdays coming up, birthday twins and star signs</li>
<li>Match people to your contacts and save their birthdays into your contacts</li>
<li>Message templates with {name}, {fullname} and {age}</li>
<li>Color themes, light and dark mode, in English and Spanish</li>
<li>No internet permission, no tracking; everything stays on your phone</li>
</ul>
